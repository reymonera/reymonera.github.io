Years ago I gave a webinar called "*Conceptos básicos en evolución Pokemon*". This might have been the biggest event I have ever done in science communication, and I'm forever grateful with the Sociedad Peruana de Astrobiología del Perú for allowing this idea to see the light. I still have no idea how we managed it, but it has been an incredible journey that culminated with people asking to read scientific literature. Basically, true science communication happened.
